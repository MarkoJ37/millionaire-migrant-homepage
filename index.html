How One Canadian Family Built Their Global Plan B

A Canadian entrepreneur in his late 30s came to Millionaire Migrant with one goal: create greater freedom and security for his family.

The ChallengeThe family wanted more flexibility, stronger asset protection, and a future that was not tied to a single country.

The StrategyWe created a multi-jurisdiction strategy that separated their residency, citizenship, banking, and asset structures.

The plan combined private banking, international investment opportunities, and a European Golden Visa pathway. This gave the family greater flexibility while supporting long-term growth and future planning.

The OutcomeToday, the family has greater control over where they live, how they invest, and the opportunities available to the next generation.

What began as a desire for lower taxes became a complete global Plan B.

How One Australian Entrepreneur Restructured His Global Tax Position

An Australian entrepreneur came to Millionaire Migrant to establish a more tax-efficient international base without disrupting the business and investments he had already built.

The ChallengeHis business remained anchored in Australia, leaving his tax exposure tied to a single system regardless of where he chose to live.

The StrategyWith direct input from Jeremy Savory and our senior advisory team, we developed a coordinated strategy across relocation, tax residency, corporate structuring, and asset protection.

We guided his transition to UAE tax residency, established a new foreign tax-free business entity, and moved his investments into an offshore trust. This separated his personal, operating, and investment structures for the first time.

The OutcomeToday, he holds UAE tax residency, operates through a Hong Kong entity, and holds his investments through an offshore trust. Through six months of post-implementation concierge support, our team coordinated the transition, supported the client through the move, and helped ensure every part of the new structure was put in place correctly.
